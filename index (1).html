Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about interim finance consulting

What does an interim CFO do?

An interim CFO provides full-scope financial leadership during transitions such as a CFO departure, organizational restructuring, or rapid growth. This includes managing the finance team, owning board reporting and monthly close processes, maintaining stakeholder communication, and building the infrastructure and documentation needed for a permanent hire to succeed. At Control Group B, interim CFO engagements typically last 3 to 9 months.

What is a Quality of Earnings analysis?

A Quality of Earnings (QoE) analysis is a financial due diligence process used during M&A transactions to verify the sustainability and accuracy of a company's reported earnings. It identifies non-recurring items, adjusts for accounting irregularities, and provides buyers or investors with a normalized view of financial performance. Control Group B has supported QoE analyses for PE-backed transactions exceeding $170 million in value.

When should a company hire an FP&A consultant?

Companies typically hire an FP&A consultant when their financial reporting can no longer support leadership decision-making — for example, when the board is asking questions the current reporting cannot answer, when budgets and forecasts are built in disconnected spreadsheets, or when a PE sponsor requires more rigorous financial planning. An FP&A consultant builds the models, dashboards, and planning cadences that give leadership confidence in the numbers.

What is the difference between a fractional CFO and an interim CFO?

A fractional CFO works part-time on an ongoing basis, typically a few days per week or month, providing strategic financial guidance to companies that do not need or cannot afford a full-time CFO. An interim CFO fills a full-time role temporarily, usually during a leadership transition, and handles the complete scope of CFO responsibilities including team management, board reporting, and close processes. Control Group B provides interim CFO services where full-time, hands-on leadership is required during transitions.

How do you help PE-backed companies with financial reporting?

PE-backed companies face specific reporting demands from their sponsors, including monthly operating reports, KPI dashboards, variance analysis, and board-ready financial packages. Control Group B builds automated reporting infrastructure using Alteryx, Tableau, SQL, and ERP integration to deliver consistent, accurate financial data. We have managed unified FP&A across as many as 7 entities in a single PE-backed roll-up.

Frequently asked questions

Common questions about finance consulting

What is fractional FP&A and how does it work?

A senior finance professional runs your planning and analysis function for a flat monthly fee, typically 10 to 30+ hours a month: forecasts, board reporting, KPI dashboards, variance analysis and the budget cycle. It suits companies that need a Director of Finance or VP of FP&A but not full-time.

What does an interim CFO do?

An interim CFO provides full-scope financial leadership during a transition: managing the finance team, owning board reporting and the monthly close, and building what a permanent hire needs to succeed. Our interim engagements typically last 3 to 9 months.

What is the difference between a fractional CFO and an interim CFO?

A fractional CFO works part-time on an ongoing basis. An interim CFO fills the full-time role temporarily, usually during a leadership transition. Control Group B provides interim CFO services, where full-time, hands-on leadership is required.

What is a Quality of Earnings analysis?

Financial due diligence used in M&A to verify that reported earnings are accurate and sustainable. It identifies non-recurring items and gives buyers a normalized view of performance. We have supported QoE analysis on a $170M PE-backed transaction.

When should a company hire an FP&A consultant?

When reporting can no longer support leadership decisions: the board asks questions the reporting can't answer, budgets live in disconnected spreadsheets, or a PE sponsor requires more rigorous planning.

How do you help PE-backed companies with financial reporting?

Sponsors expect monthly operating reports, KPI dashboards, variance analysis and board-ready packages. We build automated reporting with Alteryx, Tableau, SQL and ERP integration, and have run unified FP&A across as many as 7 entities in one roll-up.

Does Control Group B provide investment banking services?

Control Group B is a finance consulting firm, not a licensed investment bank or broker-dealer. For clients whose needs extend beyond operational finance and into the need to raise capital for growth or to execute a merger/acquisition, we refer them to Cappello Global, LLC — a fully licensed boutique investment bank headquartered in Los Angeles with over 50 years of transaction experience across 60+ countries. Securities transactions are cleared through Camden Financial Services, a FINRA member Broker-Dealer. Control Group B prepares the financial infrastructure — models, data rooms, reporting — that makes clients transaction-ready, and Cappello Global handles the capital markets execution as a separate engagement.
